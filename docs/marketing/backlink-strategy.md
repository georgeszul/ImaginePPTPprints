# Backlink and local search strategy

This plan gets Imagine PPTP Prints found by people in and around Elgin who search for custom shirts, banners, business cards or stickers, in English or Spanish. For a local print shop, most of the value comes from three things: a complete Google Business Profile, the same business details everywhere, and links that real customers and local groups give you because you printed something for them. Buying links or joining link schemes can get the site penalized, so this plan never does that.

## 1. Use the same business details everywhere

Search engines trust a business when its name, address and phone match exactly across the web. Copy these lines character for character into every listing:

- **Name**: Imagine PPTP Prints
- **Address**: 840 N State St, Elgin, IL 60123
- **Phone**: (331) 255-5330
- **Website**: the live domain, with `https://`
- **Hours**: the same hours the site shows (currently "open 24 hours, every day"; change both together if that changes)

## 2. Week 1: claim the big listings

These listings come first, because people and search engines use them most:

1. **Google Business Profile**: claim or create it at business.google.com. Pick "Print shop" as the main category and add "T-shirt store", "Screen printer" and "Sign shop" if they fit. Add the services, 10 or more real order photos, the website link and the quote page link. Answer every review.
2. **Bing Places for Business**: import the Google profile.
3. **Apple Business Connect**: for Apple Maps and Siri.
4. **Facebook page**: make sure it links to the website and shows the same address and phone.
5. **Yelp** and **Nextdoor business page**: both are common for local services.

## 3. Weeks 2–4: local citations

Add the business to a few trusted directories. Quality beats quantity; skip any directory that asks for payment to "boost" links:

- Elgin Area Chamber of Commerce (member directory, if you join)
- Better Business Bureau
- Yellow Pages (yp.com) and Manta
- Thumbtack (people search there for custom printing quotes)
- Spanish-language business directories and Hispanic chambers of commerce in the Chicago area

## 4. Ongoing: earn links with real work

These links take more effort, but they are the ones that move rankings:

- **Ask happy customers for a credit line.** When you print shirts for a team, a business or an event, ask them to add "Shirts by Imagine PPTP Prints" with a link on their website, event page or social posts.
- **Sponsor local teams and events.** Youth sports teams, school clubs, church events, fiestas and 5K runs usually list sponsors with a link. Printing their shirts at a discount often earns that spot.
- **Pitch local news.** The Courier-News and the Daily Herald run small-business stories. A bilingual print shop that turns orders around in 24–48 hours is a good local angle.
- **Partner pages.** Ask suppliers, local designers and photographers you work with to list you as a print partner, and link back to them.
- **Share the useful pages.** The price calculator, the size charts and the file-format FAQ answer questions people ask in local Facebook groups. Share the page link when it answers someone's question.

## 5. What to avoid

None of these help a local shop, and several break Google's rules:

- Buying links, link packages or "guest posts" sold as SEO
- Link exchanges ("link to me and I'll link to you") at scale
- Directories that exist only to sell links
- Fake or paid reviews, or reviews written by the owner or staff

## 6. Monthly check (15 minutes)

| Check | Where |
| --- | --- |
| New and lost links | Google Search Console → Links |
| Search terms and clicks | Google Search Console → Performance |
| Calls, direction requests, website visits | Google Business Profile → Performance |
| Visitors and where they came from | Netlify → Analytics |
| New reviews to answer | Google, Facebook, Yelp |
